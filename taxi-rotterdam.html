Taxi from Schiphol to Rotterdam

Seventy kilometres, about fifty minutes. One road for most of it, and the taximeter decides the fare — nothing is added for the distance.

On the road

The A4 carries you past Leiden and The Hague and hands you to the A13 outside Delft. Rotterdam was rebuilt wide, so unlike the older cities a car reaches the door of most addresses, whether that is Blaak, Kop van Zuid or a street in Kralingen.

Going back to Schiphol

Going back, allow ninety minutes for a flight out of Schiphol: fifty on the road and forty for the queue at security, which on a Monday morning is the part people underestimate.

What you pay

The ride is on the taximeter, within the national maximum tariff, and you pay what it reads at the door. The €20 booking fee is separate and is not deducted from the fare. No surge pricing, and nothing extra for night, luggage or the airport itself.
