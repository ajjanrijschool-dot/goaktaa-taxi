How the fare works

Three states of the meter.

AT PICKUP

The meter starts

Your driver switches the meter to bezet the moment you get in. Waiting time at the terminal is on us for the first 30 minutes after landing.

EN ROUTE

Time and distance

The meter counts kilometres and minutes together, at the tariff printed on the window sticker. Traffic on the A4 changes the number. Nobody changes it by hand.

ON ARRIVAL

You pay the reading

Meter stops, receipt prints. Card, cash or company invoice. Tolls and parking, if any, are listed separately on the receipt.

Dutch taxis run on government-set maximum tariffs, and ours sit inside them. The sticker in the rear window shows the exact rate before you step in. Need a fixed price for a long transfer instead? Ask the dispatcher.

Meeting point

Arrivals 4, past the coffee bar.

Schiphol has one arrivals hall with four exits. Come out at Arrivals 4 and look for a driver holding a card with your name on it. If you surface at a different exit, call the number on your confirmation text and the car moves to you.
