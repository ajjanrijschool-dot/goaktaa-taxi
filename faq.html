Questions

Questions passengers ask

If something here is not answered, call the number at the top of the page. Somebody picks up around the clock.

Where do I meet my driver at Schiphol?

Schiphol has one arrivals hall with four exits. Tell us which one when you book — Arrivals 1, 2, 3 or 4 — and your driver stands there holding a card with your name on it. If you are not sure, put down Schiphol Plaza and call the number on your confirmation when you land.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We watch the flight number you gave us, so the pickup moves with the plane. Land early or late and the car is there for the real landing time, not the printed one. The first 30 minutes of waiting after you land are free.

How much does the ride cost?

Whatever the taximeter reads when you arrive, within the maximum taxi tariff set by the Dutch government. The rate is on a sticker in the rear window before you get in. There is no surge pricing and nothing extra for night, luggage or the airport itself. Tolls and parking, if there are any, appear separately on the receipt.

What is the €20 booking fee for?

It holds a car and a driver for your time slot, and it is paid online when you book. It is not part of the fare and is not deducted from it. Because a held slot cannot be resold at short notice, the €20 is not refunded if you cancel.

Can I have a fixed price instead of the meter?

For a long ride, call the dispatch number and ask. Anything agreed is agreed before you get in, never afterwards. For ordinary Schiphol runs the meter is usually the cheaper of the two, which is why we lead with it.

How do I pay for the ride itself?

At the door, when you arrive: card, cash, or on a company invoice. The meter stops, the receipt prints, and that is the end of it.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Change the time free of charge up to 2 hours before pickup — call or message the number on your confirmation. You can cancel whenever you like and the ride costs you nothing further, but the €20 booking fee stays with us.

Is my ride confirmed the moment I submit the form?

No. The form sends us a request. The ride is confirmed when we come back to you, which we do as fast as we can and always before your pick-up time.

How many passengers fit in the car?

Four. We drive saloon cars, so four passengers with their luggage is the limit. Larger groups are no problem — put the real number on the form and we send a second car, which we confirm with you by phone.

How much luggage can I bring?

Tell us the real number of suitcases on the booking form. Four passengers with four large cases is tight in a saloon car, and it is better that we know before we set off than at the kerb — if a second car is needed we will say so.

Do you have child seats?

Yes, but we need notice — mention it in the notes when you book, not on the day. A seat has to be collected and fitted before the driver sets off, so a same-day request cannot always be met.

Can I bring my pet?

Small animals travel in a carrier, and please say so when you book. Guide and assistance dogs are always welcome.

Do you drive outside the Netherlands?

No. We drive inside the Netherlands only. For Belgium, Germany or further, another operator will serve you better than we can.

Do you drive at night?

Yes, around the clock, and there is no night surcharge. A 04:00 pickup for a first flight is an ordinary morning for us.

Can I book a ride for somebody else?

Yes. Book with your own phone number and email so we can reach you, and put the passenger’s name in the notes. That is the name the driver will hold up at the exit.

Are you a licensed taxi company?

Yes. Taxi Service Go Aktaa holds a KIWA taxi permit and is registered with the Kamer van Koophandel under number 93594909. We are an independent operator and are not affiliated with Royal Schiphol Group.

Still not sure? Book the ride and put your question in the notes — we read every one.
